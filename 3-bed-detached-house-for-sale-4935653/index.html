Description

This property offers a charming setting, being within less than 10 minute drive to Waterville village, beach and the world famous Golf Course. The road outside the property is a quiet residential road, The property is a storey and half stone build house with various outbuildings set upon Circa 0.379 of an acre. The residence does require modernizing, it would qualify for the Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant.

Water well to be drilled, Electricity to be re connected, septic tank on site.

Property has 3 bedrooms, and has some original features still in place. From the rear of the property there is wonderful views of the countryside and of the Inny River below the property which is a popular salmon and sea trout river. This would make a lovely holiday retirement or a main home.
